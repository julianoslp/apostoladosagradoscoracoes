MARY'S MARRIAGE

- Accomplished the last act for the perfect marriage, in agreement with the designs saints of GOD, with many parties has gone celebrated Joseph's Marriage and Mary.

In that time the Marriage was constituted of three parts: Betrothal (was the Engagement), Conduction (intermediate period) and the Wedding (the Marriage).

In the Betrothal was made the Contract of Marriage that could be written or oral. In both cases, in the presence of witness because in that moment they combined what the bride would receive from gift.

By the law, among the Betrothal (Engagement) and the Celebration of the Wedding or Nuptials could elapse an interval of team until of twelve month. This period was called Conduction. In this period, they live separated but they could use matrimony's rights, because the Marriage�s Contract guarantees them ownership in the strict sense of the word. If they had children in this period, the offspring was considered legitimate.

On the Celebration�s day of the Wedding, at afternoon, the bridegroom went into bride's house with gala clothes accompanied by friends and musicians. Then, the retinue was formed with great pomp and joy. At the same time, groups of virgins danced and sang verses, bringing in one hand a garland of flowers and in the other hand a lamp. Cymbals, drums, fifes and flutes roared enthusiastically to invite people to participate in the same happiness. The climax of all Marriage was the banquet. When a married couple surpassed the door to enter in their home, began the Matrimonial Banquet. Generally, the party continued per one week, and it was always motive of many congratulations among the participants. Certainly, Joseph's Marriage and MARY was of the same way.
