We invited you to pray like this:

 

"HOLY SPIRIT"

 

GIVE ME A BIG HEART,

opened to Your silent and strong inspiring word;

closed at all stingy ambitions ;

indifferent to any worthless human competition;

convinced of the Holy Church's sense!

 

A VERY BIG HEART,

Wishful of to become similar at the HEART OF THE LORD JESUS!

 

A BIG AND GENTLE HEART FOR,

that it have the own desire to reward the charming tenderness and the wonderful love of the BLESSED MOTHER'S IMMACULATE HEART!

 

A BIG, GENTLE AND STRONG HEART FOR,

to love the all, to serve and to suffer for everybody!

 

A BIG, GENTLE, STRONG AND MERCIFUL HEART FOR,

to overcome all the probations, all tedium, all fatigues, every disillusions and every offenses!

 

ABOVE ALL, GIVE ME A BIG AND STRONG HEART FOR,

to persevere until the sacrifice, if necessary;
a heart whose larger happiness is to palpitate of joy with CHRIST'S HEART, always executing with humility and fidelity the ETERNAL FATHER'S Wish!

 

Pope Paul VI
