"APOSTOLATE OF THE SACRED HEARTS"

  - Humanity was not born by chance and even it not descends from some animal. We were born special creatures according to a prescribed and idealized plan in the Heart of the CREATOR, to live in plentitude, to be happy and to correspond as a son to the Divine Love.

It means to say that were born for a sublime purpose and not for we live egoistically, essentially worried with the work, in possessing many goods, founding the personal success in the material conquest, in the external appearance and in to enjoy the pleasures of the life and to satisfy all the desires and inmost thoughts.

Every human has a Body and Soul. The Body is born from the relationship of our parents, blessed by the Sacrament of Matrimony. The Soul comes from GOD. In the sacred moment of fertilization, the Soul, the fruit from the Will of the CREATOR, penetrates in the fertilized egg in the mother's uterus, orientating the cells multiplication and giving form to the fetus, which is a creature that it go to be born to life.

Therefore, each person has a human part, which is the Body, and a Divine part, which is the Soul, invention of GOD. They are two essential parts and nobody exists without them.

Because of this, a person to be "complete" and to have a life in plenitude has to harmonize his existence, equally satisfying the necessities of the Body and the Soul.

We satisfy Body necessities every time that we attend its exigencies, for example: when we are hungry and eat, when we are thirsty and drink, and so successively, when we practice sports, we do recreation, we sleep, etc.

We satisfy the Soul necessities to looking for its union with GOD, through all the forms of Prayer and Good Works.

If a person only worries about knowing the Body needs, the Soul itself "atrophies". Its existence will be abnormal, because it will not be complete, even if apparently one demonstrates financial progress. By the reason which there being two vital parts (Body and Soul) is being exercised only one.

Of the same way that Body does not live without material alimentation, the Soul "does not live" without spiritual alimentation (prayer and good works).  In other words, the Soul does not have "life in plenitude" without primordial food which is the presence of GOD. She only exists ... And a Body with a "lifeless" Soul is like a person with a "body part atrophied".

These considerations propose that, for a person to have stable equilibrium morally and physically it must to have equally care and zeal with both, to his Body and Soul.

Consequently, searching to live in perfect consonance with own nature, it will also be in internal harmony with life, will be a happy person. You will have difficulties and tribulations, as all we have, inherent the Cross and the Mission of the existence that LORD trusted to us. But we will have also the inspiration, the tranquility and the discernment necessary to find adequate solution to all problems, because your Soul will receive the talent of the HOLY GHOST in the Light of GOD.

All this, without fear and without reserves or preoccupations, above all, because must to exist in a person that believes, a solid perception, built on the reality that the LORD GOD accomplishes your works and does not abandon them, HE administers, helps, enlivens and sanctifies all who seek HIS special and powerful protection.

- "Love yourselves and one another as I have loved you".(John 15.12)

Whom has faith and believe decide to put into practice JESUS' Words. We were born to love and whom loves it does not kill, does not rob, does not commit adultery, keeps away the ambition which involves the heart, looking for to be patient and living a fraternal way as true brothers which we are, sons of the same and marvelous ETERNAL FATHER.

GOD created us and permitted that we exist for life. One day, concluded the mission which HE trusted us and which we have to complete with disposition, discernment and courage despite all obstacles, we return to HIS the loving arms to receive definitively the inheritance of HIS eternal Love.
